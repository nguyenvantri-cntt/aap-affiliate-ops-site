Privacy Policy

AAP Affiliate Ops · Last updated: 2026-11-13

This policy explains how AAP Affiliate Ops (“we”) handles data when you use the Service to manage TikTok affiliate publishing workflows.

Data we process: account email/name for login; TikTok OAuth tokens and basic profile fields you authorize; product links, captions, media files you upload; and operational metrics you enter.

How we use it: to authenticate you, connect TikTok Login Kit / Content Posting API with your consent, generate draft captions, prepare publish packages, and send optional Telegram notifications you configure.

Sharing: TikTok receives data required for OAuth and posting APIs you enable. We do not sell personal data. Tokens are stored encrypted at rest when configured.

Retention & rights: You may request deletion of your account data by emailing nguyenvantri.cntt@gmail.com. Revoke TikTok access anytime in TikTok app settings.
